# Incident Response Procedure

Production incidents must be assessed according to impact
and severity.

SEV1 incidents represent critical service disruption and
require immediate escalation.

SEV2 incidents represent major degradation affecting
customers or important business processes.

The incident commander coordinates investigation,
communication and recovery activities.

Relevant logs, metrics, deployment history and dependency
health should be collected during investigation.

After service recovery, significant incidents should have
a post-incident review.
